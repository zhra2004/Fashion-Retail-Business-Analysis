# 👗 Gayanara – Fashion Retail Business Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Gayanara Fashion Retail Business Analysis is a data analytics project focused on evaluating business performance in the fashion retail industry. This project analyzes sales transactions, customer data, product reviews, and inventory records to identify business trends, understand customer behavior, and evaluate stock performance.

Using Microsoft Excel, Power Query, and Power Pivot, the project transforms raw datasets into interactive dashboards and actionable business insights to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze sales performance to identify top-performing products and categories.
- Evaluate customer behavior and product ratings to understand customer experience.
- Monitor inventory levels to identify potential stock management issues.
- Generate actionable insights and recommendations to improve retail business performance.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel:** Data analysis and dashboard development.
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning, transformation, and integration.
- **Power Pivot:** Data modeling and relationship management.

## 📂 Dataset

The project uses five main datasets:

- Orders
- Order Items
- Customers
- Reviews
- Transactions

The datasets were processed and integrated using Power Query to support comprehensive business analysis.

## 📊 Dashboard & Key Analysis

### 1. 💰 Sales Performance Dashboard

Analyzes revenue, order volume, units sold, product performance, category performance, and monthly sales trends.

### 2. 👥 Customer Analysis Dashboard

Evaluates customer-related metrics and product reviews to understand customer feedback and product satisfaction.

### 3. 📦 Stock Analysis Dashboard

Monitors inventory levels across products and categories to evaluate stock distribution and identify potential inventory management issues.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Jackets generated the highest category revenue, reaching IDR 266.94 million.
- Dress products recorded the highest inventory level, with 5,585 units, followed by jackets with 5,238 units.
- Jacket Denim Ratu Mode was identified as the most purchased product.
- Syal Rajut Sandangindo received the highest product rating.
- Customer review coverage was relatively low, indicating an opportunity to improve feedback collection.

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

- Prioritize high-performing products and categories to optimize sales strategies.
- Improve customer feedback collection to gain better insights into customer satisfaction.
- Evaluate inventory allocation based on product sales performance and demand patterns.
- Monitor product performance regularly to support better inventory planning.

## 🏆 Project Outcome

This project demonstrates the application of data cleaning, data transformation, data modeling, exploratory analysis, and dashboard development using Microsoft Excel.

The final dashboards provide an overview of sales performance, customer feedback, and inventory conditions, helping translate raw business data into meaningful insights and actionable recommendations.
